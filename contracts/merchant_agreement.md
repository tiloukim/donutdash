# DonutDash Merchant Agreement

**Effective Date:** [EFFECTIVE_DATE]
**Version:** [VERSION]

This Merchant Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into by and between **Kimco LLC**, a Texas limited liability company doing business as "DonutDash" (Texas DBA #803302557), with its principal place of business in Tyler, Texas ("**DonutDash**"), and the merchant identified in the executed Order Form, account onboarding form, or electronic acceptance record incorporated herein by reference ("**Merchant**"). DonutDash and Merchant are each a "**Party**" and collectively the "**Parties**."

By signing the Order Form, accepting these terms electronically, or activating a merchant account on the DonutDash Platform, Merchant agrees to be bound by this Agreement.

---

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Customer**" means an end user who places an Order through the Platform.

1.2 "**Driver**" means an independent contractor who uses the Platform to perform delivery services.

1.3 "**Gross Order Value**" or "**GOV**" means the price charged to a Customer for the Merchant Items, exclusive of Delivery Fee, Service Fee, sales tax, and tips.

1.4 "**Marketplace Facilitator**" has the meaning given in Texas Tax Code §151.0242.

1.5 "**Merchant Items**" means the food, beverages, and other goods Merchant offers for sale through the Platform.

1.6 "**Order**" means a Customer's request to purchase Merchant Items through the Platform.

1.7 "**Platform**" means the DonutDash websites, mobile applications, merchant tablet software, APIs, and related services.

1.8 "**Stripe**" means Stripe, Inc., the third-party payment processor used by DonutDash to facilitate payments and payouts.

## 2. The Platform and the Parties' Roles

2.1 **Technology Platform.** DonutDash operates a technology platform that connects Customers with independent local merchants and independent contractor drivers. DonutDash is not a restaurant, food service operator, or delivery company.

2.2 **Merchant as Seller.** Merchant is the seller of all Merchant Items and is solely responsible for their preparation, ingredients, packaging, allergen disclosure, and compliance with all applicable food-safety, health, and labeling laws and regulations.

2.3 **Independent Contractors.** Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, employment, agency, or franchise relationship between Merchant and DonutDash. Each Party is an independent contractor.

## 3. Onboarding and Required Information

3.1 Merchant shall provide the following accurate and current information to be onboarded:

(a) Legal business name and any DBAs;
(b) Federal Employer Identification Number (EIN) or, if applicable, taxpayer identification number;
(c) Business address and contact information;
(d) Verified business bank account for receipt of payouts via Stripe Connect Express;
(e) Copies of food-handler certifications required under Texas law and any local ordinances;
(f) A current Certificate of Liability Insurance evidencing coverage that meets the requirements of Section 12 and naming Kimco LLC as additional insured upon request;
(g) Any additional documentation reasonably requested by DonutDash to verify Merchant's identity, eligibility, and compliance.

3.2 Merchant authorizes DonutDash and its third-party verification providers to verify the information submitted, including conducting background checks on the business and its principals where permitted by law.

## 4. Menu, Pricing, and Order Acceptance

4.1 **Menu Control.** Merchant retains sole control over its menu, including item descriptions, photographs, allergen disclosures, and prices. DonutDash does not modify Merchant's menu or pricing without Merchant's authorization.

4.2 **Price Parity.** Merchant agrees that prices shown on the Platform will be no higher than prices Merchant offers for the same items at its physical location, unless Merchant provides DonutDash with written notice of a delivery-specific menu and the rationale therefor.

4.3 **Order Acceptance Window.** Merchant shall accept or decline each incoming Order within five (5) minutes of receipt. Orders not accepted within this window may be cancelled or re-routed automatically. Repeated failure to accept Orders may result in suspension of Merchant's account.

4.4 **Operating Hours.** Merchant shall keep its operating hours on the Platform accurate and shall pause its store on the Platform when it is unable to fulfill Orders.

## 5. Commission and Payouts

5.1 **Commission Tiers.** DonutDash will deduct a commission from the Gross Order Value of each Order at the following rates:

| Tier | Eligibility | Commission |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | Default | 20% of GOV |
| Volume | 50 or more completed Orders in the prior calendar week | 18% of GOV |
| Anchor | 100 or more completed Orders in the prior calendar week | 16% of GOV |

Tier eligibility is recalculated weekly. DonutDash may, in its discretion, offer custom commission rates by separate written addendum.

5.2 **Payout Schedule.** DonutDash shall pay Merchant the Gross Order Value of completed Orders, less commission and any applicable adjustments, on a weekly basis each Friday, by ACH deposit through Stripe Connect Express to Merchant's verified bank account.

5.3 **No Onboarding, Subscription, or Termination Fees.** DonutDash does not charge an onboarding fee, monthly subscription fee, or termination fee.

5.4 **Adjustments.** DonutDash may offset against future payouts amounts owed by Merchant due to refunds, chargebacks, fraudulent Orders, missing or incorrect items, or breach of this Agreement.

5.5 **Stripe Terms.** By accepting payouts through Stripe Connect Express, Merchant agrees to be bound by the Stripe Connected Account Agreement and other Stripe terms applicable to Connected Accounts.

## 6. Customer-Facing Fees Collected by DonutDash

For transparency, Customers are charged the following amounts in addition to the Gross Order Value at checkout:

(a) Delivery Fee — $3.99 within the standard service radius (3 miles from Merchant);
(b) Service Fee — 10% of GOV;
(c) Small Order Fee — $1.99 if GOV is less than $10.00;
(d) Sales tax — at applicable rates (see Section 7);
(e) Tip — Customer-selected; 100% of tip is paid to the Driver.

These fees are retained by DonutDash (or, in the case of tips, paid through to Drivers) and are not part of Merchant's payout.

## 7. Marketplace Facilitator and Sales Tax [ATTORNEY REVIEW REQUIRED]

7.1 **Marketplace Facilitator Status.** DonutDash is a "marketplace provider" within the meaning of Texas Tax Code §151.0242 and acts as a "marketplace facilitator" with respect to sales of Merchant Items made through the Platform.

7.2 **Collection and Remittance.** DonutDash shall (a) calculate and collect Texas state and local sales and use tax ("**Sales Tax**") on each Order at the rate applicable to the delivery location; (b) certify to Merchant in writing (which may be by electronic record) that DonutDash assumes the rights and duties of a seller with respect to such Sales Tax; and (c) report and remit such Sales Tax to the Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts in accordance with Texas Tax Code §151.0242.

7.3 **Effect on Merchant.** As permitted by Texas Tax Code §151.0242(c), Merchant is not required to collect or remit Sales Tax on the portion of an Order facilitated by DonutDash through the Platform, and Merchant may exclude such sales from its Texas sales tax return to the extent permitted by the Texas Comptroller's published guidance. Merchant remains responsible for Sales Tax on sales made outside the Platform and for all other taxes (including income, franchise, and employment taxes) arising from its business.

7.4 **Records and Cooperation.** Each Party shall maintain records of Orders sufficient to substantiate the Sales Tax position taken under this Section. Merchant shall cooperate with DonutDash in responding to any audit or inquiry from the Texas Comptroller or other taxing authority.

7.5 **Indemnity for Sales Tax Position.** DonutDash shall indemnify Merchant against any Sales Tax, penalties, and interest assessed against Merchant by the Texas Comptroller solely on account of Sales Tax that DonutDash was required to but failed to collect or remit under Texas Tax Code §151.0242 with respect to Orders fulfilled through the Platform. This Section 7.5 does not cover assessments arising from Merchant's misclassification of items, incorrect pricing, sales made outside the Platform, or other Merchant errors.

## 8. Tax Reporting

DonutDash will issue Merchant an IRS Form 1099-K for each calendar year in which Merchant meets the applicable IRS reporting thresholds. Merchant is responsible for all federal, state, and local income, franchise, and self-employment taxes arising from its business and acknowledges that the amounts reported on Form 1099-K may differ from Merchant's net payout due to commissions, refunds, and other adjustments described in this Agreement.

## 9. Refunds and Customer Issues

9.1 DonutDash administers customer refunds in accordance with the customer-facing refund policy posted on the Platform from time to time.

9.2 Merchant authorizes DonutDash to issue partial or full refunds to Customers and to offset such refunds against Merchant's payouts where the cause of the refund is reasonably attributable to Merchant (including missing or incorrect items, food-quality complaints, Order rejection after preparation, or failure to meet promised preparation times).

9.3 Refunds attributable to causes outside Merchant's control (Driver fault, Platform fault, Customer cancellation within the no-charge window) shall not be offset against Merchant's payouts.

## 10. Compliance with Law

Merchant shall, at its sole expense, obtain and maintain all licenses, permits, registrations, and approvals required to operate its business and to prepare and sell Merchant Items, including all health-department permits, food-handler certifications, and business licenses required by the State of Texas, Smith County, and the City of Tyler (or other applicable jurisdictions). Merchant shall comply with all applicable laws, rules, and regulations, including food-safety, labeling, allergen-disclosure, consumer-protection, anti-discrimination, and employment laws.

Merchant shall maintain, at its expense, at least the following insurance coverages with carriers rated A- or better by A.M. Best:

(c) Such other coverage as is customary for Merchant's business.

Merchant shall furnish a Certificate of Insurance evidencing the foregoing coverages upon request and shall name Kimco LLC as additional insured under the Commercial General Liability policy if reasonably requested.

## 13. Term; Suspension; Termination

13.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the date Merchant first accepts it and continues until terminated under this Section.

13.2 **Termination for Convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, on seven (7) days' written notice (which may be by email).

13.3 **Suspension.** DonutDash may immediately suspend Merchant's access to the Platform if Merchant (a) breaches this Agreement; (b) presents a risk to Customer health, safety, or satisfaction; (c) fails to maintain required licenses or insurance; or (d) becomes the subject of suspected fraud, regulatory action, or insolvency proceedings.

13.4 **Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, Merchant's access to the Platform will end. DonutDash shall remit any final payout owed to Merchant (less applicable adjustments) on the next regularly scheduled payout date. Sections 7, 8, 11, 14, 15, 16, 17, and 18 shall survive termination.
