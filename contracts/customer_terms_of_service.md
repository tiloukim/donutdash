## 4. How Orders Work

When you place an order through the Platform:

1. You select items from a Merchant's menu and submit an order.
2. We transmit the order to the Merchant. The Merchant has approximately five (5) minutes to accept; if they do not, the order may be cancelled and any pre-authorized charge released.
3. Once accepted, the Merchant prepares the food and a Driver picks it up and delivers it to the address you provided.
4. Estimated arrival times are estimates only and are not guaranteed.

DonutDash is not the seller of the food. The Merchant is the seller and is responsible for food preparation, ingredients, allergens, packaging quality, and accuracy of menu descriptions.

## 5. Pricing and Fees

The total you pay at checkout will include:

- **Item subtotal** — set by the Merchant.
- **Delivery fee** — $3.99 flat for deliveries within our standard service radius (3 miles from the Merchant) at launch.
- **Service fee** — 10% of the item subtotal. This helps fund Platform operations, customer support, and technology.
- **Small order fee** — $1.99 if your item subtotal is less than $10.00.
- **Sales tax** — calculated at the applicable rate (currently 8.25% in Tyler, Smith County, Texas: 6.25% Texas state + 2.00% local). DonutDash collects and remits sales tax as a marketplace facilitator under Texas Tax Code §151.0242.
- **Tip** — optional. The default tip is 20% and you may adjust it. **100% of your tip is paid to the Driver.**

Prices and fees may change. The total shown at checkout is what you will be charged.
