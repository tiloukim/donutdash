This Privacy Policy describes how Kimco LLC dba DonutDash ("**DonutDash**," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects information about you when you use our website at donutdash.app, our mobile applications, our merchant tablet software, or any related services (collectively, the "**Platform**"). ## 1. Who This Policy Applies To

This Privacy Policy applies to:

- **Customers** who place orders through the Platform;
- **Drivers** who perform deliveries through the Platform as independent contractors;
- **Merchants** who list and sell items through the Platform;
- Visitors to our website and other people who interact with us.

When this Policy refers to "you," it refers to all of these groups unless context indicates otherwise.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

- **Account information:** name, email address, mobile phone number, password, and (for Drivers and Merchants) date of birth, address, government identification numbers (such as Social Security number or EIN), and tax information necessary for IRS reporting (Form 1099-K and Form 1099-NEC).
- **Profile information:** profile photo, vehicle and license details (Drivers), business name, menu, photos, and bank account details (Merchants).
- **Order and transaction information:** items ordered, delivery address, special instructions, tips, and order history.
- **Payment information:** credit or debit card information (collected and stored by Stripe; we receive a token, not your full card number) and bank account information for payouts (collected by Stripe).
- **Communications:** messages you send to customer support, including the contents of voice calls handled by our AI receptionist.
- **Verification information:** background-check authorization, motor-vehicle records, and the results of those checks (Drivers).

### 2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

- **Device information:** device model, operating system, unique device identifiers, mobile network information, and crash logs.
- **Usage information:** pages and screens viewed, features used, taps and clicks, and time stamps.
- **Location information:**
  - **Customers:** approximate location (from IP address) and, with your permission, precise location to find nearby merchants and confirm delivery addresses.
  - **Drivers:** precise GPS location (foreground and, where you grant permission, background) while you are logged into the Driver app, used to match you with deliveries, route you to pickups and dropoffs, share live ETA with customers and merchants, and verify deliveries.
- **Cookies and similar technologies:** described in Section 8.

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

- **Identity and background:** from consumer-reporting agencies and identity-verification providers (Drivers).
- **Payment and payout:** from Stripe regarding the status of payments, refunds, and payouts.
- **Maps and routing:** from our mapping providers for distance, ETA, and address validation.
- **Authentication:** if you sign in using a third-party identity provider (where supported), we receive basic profile information from that provider.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Operate the Platform, process and fulfill orders, route deliveries, and calculate fees and pay;
- Authenticate users, prevent and investigate fraud, abuse, and security incidents;
- Provide customer support, including by phone, SMS, email, and in-app messaging;
- Communicate with you about your account, orders, payouts, important changes, and (with your consent) promotions;
- Comply with legal, tax, and regulatory obligations, including marketplace-facilitator sales-tax remittance, 1099 reporting, and responses to lawful requests from authorities;
- Conduct background checks and re-screen Drivers as required for safety and compliance;
- Improve, test, and develop new features of the Platform;
- Enforce our terms and protect the rights, property, and safety of DonutDash, users, and the public.

## 4. Legal Bases (Where Applicable)

Where data-protection laws require us to identify a legal basis for processing, we rely on (a) performance of our contract with you, (b) compliance with our legal obligations, (c) our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving the Platform, or (d) your consent.

## 5. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information to third parties for monetary consideration. We share information only as described below.

### 5.1 With Other Platform Participants

- We share Customer name, delivery address, contact number (often masked), and order details with the Merchant fulfilling the order and the Driver delivering it, as necessary to complete the delivery.
- We share Driver first name, vehicle, photo, and live location with the Customer and Merchant during an active delivery.

### 5.2 With Service Providers ("Processors")

We share information with vendors who perform services on our behalf under written agreements that restrict their use of the information. Categories include:

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Stripe, Inc.** | Payment processing, payouts, instant payouts, KYC, and tax reporting |
| **Twilio, Inc.** | SMS notifications, A2P 10DLC compliance, voice calls |
| **Supabase / PostgreSQL hosting** | Database hosting, authentication |
| **Vercel Inc.** | Web application hosting and edge delivery |
| **Mapping providers** | Distance calculation, geocoding, routing, ETA |
| **ElevenLabs / Anthropic (Claude API)** | AI phone receptionist transcription and response |
| **Background-check providers** (e.g., Checkr or similar) | Driver background and motor-vehicle screening |
| **Email and analytics providers** | Transactional email, product analytics |

A current list of subprocessors is available on request to privacy@donutdash.app.

### 5.3 For Legal and Safety Reasons

We may disclose information when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to (a) comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request (including from the Texas Comptroller, IRS, or law-enforcement agencies); (b) enforce our agreements; (c) detect, investigate, or prevent fraud, abuse, or security issues; or (d) protect the rights, property, or safety of DonutDash, our users, or others.

### 5.4 Business Transfers

If DonutDash is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such transfer and any change in this Policy as a result.

### 5.5 With Your Consent

We may share information for other purposes with your consent.

## 6. SMS Messaging and A2P 10DLC Consent

6.1 **Consent.** When you sign up, you may opt in to receive text messages from DonutDash. Consent is not required to make a purchase. Messages are sent through Twilio under a registered A2P 10DLC campaign.

6.2 **Message Categories.** Text messages may include order confirmations, status updates, delivery notifications, two-factor authentication codes, account alerts, and (only if you separately opt in) promotional messages. Message frequency varies based on your activity.

6.3 **Costs.** Message and data rates may apply, depending on your carrier and plan.

6.4 **Opt-Out and Help.** You may text **STOP** to any DonutDash short code or long code to unsubscribe at any time. Reply **HELP** for help, or contact support@donutdash.app.

6.5 **No Sale or Sharing of Mobile Information.** Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent are not sold or shared with any third party for promotional or marketing purposes. They are shared only with our messaging provider (Twilio) for the purpose of delivering messages to you and with Drivers and Merchants (often via masked numbers) to coordinate deliveries.

## 7. Payment Data and Third-Party Processors

DonutDash uses **Stripe, Inc.** as its payment processor. When you enter payment information, that information is collected and stored by Stripe in accordance with the Stripe Privacy Policy and the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS). DonutDash does not store full primary account numbers (PANs); we receive a token from Stripe that lets us reference your payment method without exposing card details.

Drivers and Merchants who receive payouts via Stripe Connect Express are subject to Stripe's identity verification (KYC) and the Stripe Connected Account Agreement.

## 8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage and pixels) to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, secure the Platform against fraud, measure performance, and understand how the Platform is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings, but disabling some cookies may limit Platform functionality. We do not use third-party advertising cookies on the Platform at this time. If we begin to use such cookies in the future, we will update this Policy and provide appropriate consent controls.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we may have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us at privacy@donutdash.app and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

## 10. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide the Platform, comply with our legal obligations (including tax and accounting record-retention requirements, generally up to seven years), resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When we no longer need information, we delete or de-identify it.

## 11. Data Security

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against loss, theft, unauthorized access, disclosure, and alteration. These include encryption of data in transit (TLS), restricted access to production systems, multi-factor authentication for administrative accounts, and use of reputable subprocessors that maintain industry-standard security practices. No security measures are perfect, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

- Know what categories of personal information we have collected, the sources, the business purposes, and the categories of recipients;

- Delete personal information we have collected from you, subject to exceptions;
- Correct inaccurate personal information;
- Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information;
- Limit use and disclosure of "sensitive personal information";
- Be free from retaliation for exercising your CCPA rights.

DonutDash does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined under the CCPA.

### 12.6 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of the rights above, email **privacy@donutdash.app** with the subject line "Privacy Rights Request" and include enough information for us to verify your identity. We will respond within the time required by applicable law (generally 45 days, with one extension where permitted). If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our response with the subject line "Privacy Rights Appeal."
