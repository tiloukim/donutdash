## 1. Independent Contractor Relationship [ATTORNEY REVIEW REQUIRED]

1.1 **Independent Contractor.** You and DonutDash expressly intend to enter into an independent-contractor relationship and not an employer-employee relationship. You are not an employee, agent, joint venturer, partner, or franchisee of DonutDash for any purpose. Nothing in this Agreement creates any such relationship.

1.2 **Control Over Means and Methods.** You retain sole and absolute discretion and control over the means, manner, and method by which you perform deliveries, including:

(a) **Whether to log in.** Whether and when to log into the Platform and make yourself available to receive delivery requests;
(b) **Which requests to accept.** Whether to accept, decline, or ignore any individual delivery request, with no penalty for declining a particular request;
(c) **Schedule.** Your hours of work, days of work, breaks, and time off;
(d) **Geography.** Where within the service area you choose to position yourself;
(e) **Routes.** The route you take from a merchant to a customer;
(f) **Equipment.** The vehicle, mobile device, and other equipment you use, all of which you supply at your own expense;
(g) **Other engagements.** Whether to provide delivery, transportation, or other services for any other person or platform, including direct competitors of DonutDash, simultaneously or otherwise.

1.3 **No Employment Benefits.** Because you are not an employee, you are not entitled to and waive any claim to: paid or unpaid leave; health, dental, vision, life, or disability insurance; retirement plan participation; workers' compensation coverage from DonutDash (you are responsible for your own occupational-accident or health coverage); unemployment insurance contributions from DonutDash; overtime; minimum wage; meal or rest breaks under wage-and-hour laws; or any other employee benefit.

1.4 **No Withholding.** DonutDash will not withhold federal income tax, FICA (Social Security and Medicare), state income tax (Texas has none), or any other taxes from amounts paid to you. You are solely responsible for reporting and paying all federal, state, and local income, self-employment, and any other taxes on amounts you earn under this Agreement.

1.5 **Your Acknowledgments.** You acknowledge that:

(a) You are operating an independent business that offers delivery services;
(b) You may, and are encouraged to, register and operate as a sole proprietor, LLC, or other business entity;
(c) You may incorporate the costs of vehicle ownership, fuel, insurance, and other business expenses into your independent business and may deduct them on your tax return as appropriate;
(d) DonutDash does not require exclusivity, does not assign you a territory, does not require uniforms or vehicle decals, does not provide training other than instructions necessary to use the Platform, and does not supervise the performance of any individual delivery;
(e) You bear the entrepreneurial opportunity for profit or loss based on your own decisions, including which requests to accept and how efficiently you complete them.

## 2. The Platform

DonutDash operates a software platform that connects independent contractor drivers with merchants and customers. DonutDash is a technology company. DonutDash is not a delivery company and does not itself perform deliveries.

## 3. Eligibility and Onboarding

3.1 To be eligible to use the Platform as a Driver, you must:

(a) Be at least 18 years old (or 19 if required by applicable state law);
(b) Hold a valid driver's license issued by a U.S. state for the class of vehicle you intend to use;
(c) Maintain a personal vehicle (car, truck, scooter, motorcycle, or bicycle as permitted in the service area) in safe operating condition, with current registration and required insurance;
(d) Maintain the minimum personal automobile liability insurance required by applicable law (and any higher amount required by your insurer for delivery use);
(e) Successfully complete a background check conducted by a third-party consumer reporting agency selected by DonutDash;
(f) Provide your legal name, date of birth, Social Security number, residential address, debit card or bank account, and other information necessary to verify your identity, comply with tax-reporting obligations, and enable payouts.

3.2 You authorize DonutDash and its third-party providers to obtain consumer reports, motor vehicle records, and other background information about you to evaluate your eligibility and continued eligibility, and to re-screen periodically. You will be provided any disclosures and consents required under the Fair Credit Reporting Act (15 U.S.C. §1681 et seq.) and applicable state law.

3.3 You shall promptly notify DonutDash of any change to your driver's license status, vehicle insurance, criminal history, or other information material to your eligibility.

## 4. Pay Structure

4.1 **Per-Delivery Pay.** For each completed delivery, you will earn:

(a) **Base pay** of $2.50 per delivery;
(b) **Mileage pay** of $0.75 per mile, calculated as the round-trip distance from the merchant's pickup location to the customer's delivery address (out-and-back), using the route distance returned by DonutDash's mapping provider; and
(c) **100% of any tip** the customer adds to the order. DonutDash does not retain any portion of any tip.

4.2 **Promotions and Incentives.** DonutDash may, in its sole discretion, offer additional pay, bonuses, or promotional incentives. The terms of any such promotion will be communicated through the Platform and may be changed or discontinued at any time.

4.3 **Pay Transparency.** You will be shown the estimated total pay (base + mileage + tip estimate, where available) for a delivery before you accept it. Final pay may differ if route or tip changes.

4.4 **Right to Decline.** You may decline any individual offer for any reason. Declining offers will not subject you to deactivation, although DonutDash may consider acceptance rate as one of many factors when making routing decisions.

## 5. Payouts; Stripe Connect Express; Instant Pay [ATTORNEY REVIEW REQUIRED]

5.1 **Standard Weekly Payout.** DonutDash will deposit your earned pay (less any adjustments under Section 5.5) to the bank account or debit card you connect through Stripe Connect Express on a weekly basis, each Friday, for amounts earned in the preceding pay week. There is no fee charged by DonutDash for the standard weekly payout.

5.2 **Instant Pay.** Subject to the eligibility requirements below, you may elect to receive an instant transfer of available earnings to an eligible debit card before the next regularly scheduled weekly payout ("**Instant Pay**").

(a) **Eligibility:** You must have completed at least twenty (20) deliveries through the Platform and your account must be in good standing (no open compliance, fraud, or background-check holds);
(b) **Minimum amount per Instant Pay:** $30;
(c) **Maximum amount per Instant Pay transaction:** $300;
(d) **Maximum number of Instant Pay transactions per calendar day:** 2;
(e) **Eligible payout instrument:** A debit card or bank account that supports instant transfers, as identified by Stripe.

5.3 **Instant Pay Fee Disclosure.** A fee of **1.75% of the requested Instant Pay amount** will be deducted from each Instant Pay transfer (the "**Instant Pay Fee**"). The Instant Pay Fee compensates DonutDash and its payment processor for the cost of providing real-time funds delivery, including fees imposed by the card networks and Stripe. Stripe charges DonutDash a separate fee for Instant Payouts under the Stripe Services Agreement and Stripe Connected Account Agreement; the 1.75% Instant Pay Fee is not the same as the Stripe-charged fee. By electing Instant Pay, you authorize DonutDash to deduct the Instant Pay Fee from the requested transfer amount before the funds are delivered to your debit card or bank account.

5.4 **Stripe Terms.** By using Stripe Connect Express to receive payouts, you agree to be bound by the Stripe Connected Account Agreement, the Stripe Services Agreement, and the Stripe Issuing terms (as applicable), as those agreements may be updated by Stripe from time to time. You authorize DonutDash to share with Stripe the identity, banking, and tax information necessary to enable payouts and tax reporting.

5.5 **Adjustments.** DonutDash may offset against your future earnings or payouts amounts owed by you due to:

(a) Refunds, chargebacks, or customer credits caused by your acts or omissions (e.g., undelivered orders, missing items you failed to pick up, food damage caused by mishandling);
(b) Fines, penalties, parking citations, or tolls reasonably attributable to your conduct;
(c) Repayment of advances, overpayments, or amounts paid in error;
(d) Sums owed under indemnification obligations in this Agreement.

DonutDash will provide reasonable notice of any material adjustment.

## 6. Tax Reporting

You are solely responsible for reporting all earnings, paying all federal self-employment tax, and complying with all other tax obligations.

## 7. Vehicle, Insurance, and Compliance

7.1 You shall maintain personal automobile insurance meeting the minimum financial responsibility requirements of Texas law (or applicable state law) for any vehicle you use for deliveries. You acknowledge that personal auto policies often exclude coverage for "delivery use" or "for-hire" use and that you should consult your insurer about appropriate endorsements or commercial coverage. DonutDash is not responsible for providing primary insurance for your vehicle, equipment, or activities.

7.2 You shall comply with all applicable traffic laws, vehicle-equipment requirements, food-handling and safety regulations, and any reasonable safety standards posted on the Platform.

7.3 You shall not consume alcohol or controlled substances while logged into the Platform or while performing deliveries, except medications taken in accordance with a valid prescription that does not impair safe driving.

## 8. Performance Standards

8.1 You agree to maintain the following performance standards:

(a) An average customer rating of at least 4.5 (on a 5-point scale), measured over a rolling window;
(b) A delivery completion rate of at least 90% (deliveries completed divided by deliveries accepted, excluding deliveries cancelled by the customer or merchant).

8.2 Failure to meet these standards may result in additional notices, temporary suspension, or deactivation. Performance standards apply to objective measurable outcomes only and not to the means or methods by which you perform deliveries, which remain within your control under Section 1.
